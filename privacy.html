Hatch & Hearth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Hatch & Hearth is a small virtual-pet app made by an independent developer (John T. Page, operating as Hatch & Hearth). This page explains, in plain words, what data the app handles and what it never does.

The short version: no ads, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data. If you never create an account, nothing about your nest leaves your device.

What the app stores

On your device (always). Your nest — your pets, their names, care history, and settings — is stored in your browser's local storage so the app works offline. This copy never leaves your device unless you sign in.

On our server (only if you create an account). Signing in is optional. If you do, we store:

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in links (we use passwordless "magic link" sign-in — there is no password to store).
  • Your nest data: pets and their names, species, growth and care state, care-log entries, trinkets, and care streaks — so your nest can follow you between devices.
  • Co-caretaker links: if you share your friend code with someone, or enter theirs, we store that link so you can care for each other's pets. Co-caretakers can see and care for your pets and see your care log.
  • Purchase records: what you bought and the Google Play order ID, so your purchases can be restored and honoured forever. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play — we never see or store your card details.
  • Custom Hatch descriptions: if you order a custom pet, the description you write is sent to our image-generation provider solely to create that pet's portraits.

Our server and database are hosted by Supabase, which processes this data on our behalf. Apart from Supabase (hosting) and Google Play (payments), your data is not shared with any third party.

What we never collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads are served — ever.
  • No analytics, behavioural tracking, or usage profiling.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or other device data. The app asks for none of these permissions.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete everything at any time, in either of two ways:

  • In the app: open Hatch & Hearth, go to the Log tab → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, pets, care log, purchase records, and co-caretaker links from our server, and wipes the copy stored on that device.
  • By email: write to jpidge.business@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for deletion. We will delete your data and confirm by reply.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — pets are gone for good, including purchased ones — so the app asks you to confirm before it proceeds. Data stored only on your device can also be removed at any time by clearing the app's site data in your browser or uninstalling the app.

Children

Hatch & Hearth is not directed at children under 13, and creating an account requires an email address. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and retention

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and each account's data is access-controlled so that only you — and any co-caretakers you have chosen — can reach your nest. We keep your data only while your account exists; when you delete your account, it is removed as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a fresh "last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will note it in the app.
